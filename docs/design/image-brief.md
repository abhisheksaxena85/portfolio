## 1. Policy (approved by the owner, 2026-10-14)

The visual plan §4.5 said "nothing generated, nothing stock" because the earlier AI phone mockups showed fictional apps with garbled text. The owner has since allowed generated images under these limits, now written into `DESIGN.md` §Imagery:

- Abstract isometric blueprint art only: blocks, lines, planes, grids.
- No text, numbers, logos, people, faces, UI screens, charts with values, or client and product claims.
- Real photographs of the team and office are **not** generated. Two records are `real-photo` and wait for the owner.

**Hand-off:** no generation tool is available to the agent. Run `yarn image:prompts` to regenerate [`image-prompts.md`](./image-prompts.md) from the manifest, then paste each prompt into the tool of your choice.

## 2. Look

Same direction as the site (Night Workshop with a Blueprint layer): near-black navy canvas, thin luminous cobalt outlines, faint grid paper, one soft glow behind one focal object. Lifecycle accents match the design tokens: **build = cobalt only, launch = amber on one small element, maintain = teal on one small element**. The full prompt text is `IMAGE_STYLE` in the manifest; `buildImagePrompt(asset)` joins style, subject, accent, composition and the avoid list into one prompt.

## 3. Sizes and files

| Format | Master ratio | Export | Used for | File type |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `hero` | 16:9 | 1600×900 | Page banners, cards (cropped to 4:3), share images (scaled into the right 55% of the OG canvas) | PNG master, under 300 KB |
| `spot` | 4:3 | 800×600 | Process steps | PNG master, under 100 KB |
| `og` | 1.91:1 | 1200×630 | Share image | PNG |

Generate at 1.5× the export size, then downscale. Keep every focal object inside the central 60% × 80% so one master serves the banner, the card and the share image. A hero master is never cropped to 1.91:1; see §6. Files go in `public/images/<group>/<id>.png`, named for what they show. PNG is the stored master: `next/image` serves AVIF or WebP from it per request, and the share-image routes (`next/og`) can only decode PNG and JPEG, so a WebP master breaks the build. Keep oversized design exports (for example the 2× OG master) in `docs/design/exports/`, never in `public/`.

## 5. Review gate for every generated image

Reject and regenerate if any of these is true:

1. Any letter, number or symbol that reads as text, even a glitch.
2. A person, hand, face or silhouette.
3. A screen that looks like a real app or dashboard.
4. A logo, store badge or vendor mark (including lookalikes).
5. Colours outside the palette, or more than one accent colour.
6. Focal object outside the central safe zone.
7. Looks like stock art or a glossy 3D render rather than a drawing.
8. Implies a result, a client or a metric the company has not confirmed.

Do not retouch a failed image with text. Fix the prompt.

## 6. Using the images on the web

- **Component:** `next/image` with the record's `file`, `width`, `height` and the `sizesAttr` for its format (`IMAGE_SIZES`). Next 16.3 docs list `preload`, `loading` and `fetchPriority`; check `node_modules/next/dist/docs/01-app/03-api-reference/02-components/image.md` before wiring, because `priority` is not in the property table.
- **Largest Contentful Paint:** the page heroes keep the SVG system diagrams, so no manifest image is an LCP candidate. Everything is lazy. If an image ever becomes the first visual on a page, load that one eagerly and nothing else.
- **Alt text:** use `alt` from the record where the image is the only visual. Use `alt=""` when it sits beside a heading that says the same thing (cards, step tiles). Never put keywords in alt.
- **Share images:** use the file convention in `node_modules/next/dist/docs/01-app/03-api-reference/03-file-conventions/01-metadata/opengraph-image.md`. Default `og-default` for every route. For section pages, scale the whole hero master into the right 55% of the 1200×630 canvas over `#070B14` and add the page title and the real lockup in the clear left 45% with `ImageResponse`. Never place the master full-bleed behind the title. The model never draws text or the logo.
- **Noindex pages still get share images:** `/industries/[slug]` is currently `noindex` (`INDUSTRIES_INDEXABLE = false`) and `/case-studies` is a placeholder. Their records stay in the manifest because links to them are still shared, but they sit behind every indexable page in generation order.
- **Guards are for the reviewer, not the model:** `buildImagePrompt()` sends style, subject, accent, composition and the avoid list only. Each subject must therefore be self-sufficient ("blank screen", "no numerals"); a guard is the check applied to the result.
- **Structured data:** once images exist, add `image` to the `Organization` and `Service` JSON-LD builders in `src/lib/schema/`, using absolute URLs from `absoluteUrl()`.
- **Motion:** none. Images are static. The only continuous animation allowed is `.hero-glow`.
- **Known polish item:** the hero masters carry their own faint grid, so on a per-goal share image the scaled master shows as a slightly lighter panel on the right. Either export future masters without the grid (the page and share card supply their own) or accept the panel; decide before the P1 batch.
- **Reuse, don't multiply:** the six goal images appear on the homepage "What do you want to achieve?" grid, the `/solutions` grid and their own pages. The three service images in `#our-work` reuse the service masters.
